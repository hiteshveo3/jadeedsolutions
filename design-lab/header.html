Aap ka faisla

Poori chaurai wali patti, koi dropdown nahi

Poori site par yahi ek header hoga. Header mein sirf 3 cheezein hain: logo, kuch links, aur ek button. Ye tasveerein neeche ke sawalon ke saath badalti hain.

  • Poori chaurai wali patti
  • Mobile: poora green page
  • Sirf 1 button
  • ✕ Dropdown ya bara menu
  • ✕ Call icon, search, doosra button

Ultra minimal ke liye main ne ye decide kiya

  • Har page par ek hi headerHar jagah same pehchan (consistency)
  • Scroll par upar chipka raheButton hamesha ek click door rehta hai
  • Header ka peechha hamesha dark greenTasveer ho ya green page, header har jagah saaf parha jaye
  • Jis page par hon, wo link lime meinVisitor ko pata rahe wo kahan hai
  • Ek hi button, gol mein teer ke saath“Free growth plan”. Round 4 wala teer.
  • Links ke saath koi teer ya nishan nahiSirf saaf alfaaz
  • Mobile par upar sirf logo + menuWhatsApp neeche wale ek button mein hai (Round 4)
  • Jo link header mein nahi, wo footer meinHeader halka rahe, koi page gum na ho
