Jadeed Marketing ki 100 behtareen cheezein — har aik ke 2 roop.

Jadeed Marketing ki 100 behtareen cheezein, naye Forest rangon mein — aur ab sab dark. Har cheez ke dono roop aik hi brand gradient par hain — farq sirf design ka. Lime sirf buttons aur numbers par, lime background kahin nahi. Sliders, tabs aur buttons chala kar dekhein.

  1. 1Har cheez par A pasand ya B pasand dabaein.
  2. 2Dono = dono alag jagah use hon. Nahi chahiye = website par na ho.
  3. 3Jo chhor dein, us mein ★ meri pasand lag jayegi. Sab ka jawab zaroori nahi.
  • Rang: Round 1
  • Font: DM Sans
  • Buttons: pill
  • Cards: safed + halki line
  • Icons: Iconsax Bulk, aik rang
  • Arrows: gol mein teer
  • Mobile: sirf 1 button
  • Site: poori dark
  • Header: patti, bina menu
